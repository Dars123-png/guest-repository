## ❓ FAQs

### What is CWL certification Bundle?

A certification bundle combines multiple CWL courses into one package, organized around a specific cybersecurity domain or learning path.

### Can i purchase more than one Bundle?

Yes. You can explore and purchase multiple bundles based on your learning requirements.

### Can i combine Bundle discounts with individual course coupons or cart discounts?

Yes! You can add individual courses and bundles to your cart. If your total cart value meets the eligibility criteria for a cart discount, the applicable discount will be applied at checkout. Discounts are calculated based on the eligible products and cart value.

### Do my lab access and exam attempts have an expiry date?

No, your exam attempts remain valid until utilised, with no expiry date. You can activate your lab access and schedule your exam whenever you're ready, at your convenience.

### Where i can access my purchased course?

Access your purchased course materials, labs, and examination details through the CWL Cyber Range Platform (CRP).

### What do i get when i purchase a certification Bundle?

Each bundle includes access to all the courses listed in the package, along with their respective study materials, practical lab access, and certification examinations.

The bundle also includes the specified number of exam attempts and lab access duration for each course. Upon successfully passing an examination, you'll receive an individual digital certificate for that course.

### What is included in the CWL all access plan?

Lifetime access to current CWL courses, 30 days of lab access, and 2 exam attempts per course. Future courses are not included.

### What is included in the CWL premium membership?

Lifetime access to current and future CWL courses, 90 days of lab access, 6 exam attempts per track, and priority support.

### Are sale courses eligible for refunds?

No, Sale courses are non-refundable. Exchanges for courses of equal value may be considered, subject to CWL’s Terms & Conditions.
